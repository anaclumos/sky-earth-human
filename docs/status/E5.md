## Minor 3, the candidate is read as NFC

- `Automaton.extends` precomposes the head of the candidate before parsing it. After GK I ARAE, `canExtend(to: "\u{1100}\u{1161}")` was false and is now true, and U+1100 U+1161 U+11A8 and U+1100 U+1161 U+AE30 are accepted the way U+AC01 and U+AC00 U+AE30 are.
- K5 measured `UITextChecker` returning NFC on all 108 Korean rows of three probe runs, so this is a guard, not a fix for a live defect.
- Deviation from the ruling, stated rather than slipped in. The ruling says to normalize the candidate at the entry of `canExtend`. Normalizing the whole candidate is linear in its length, which undoes Minor 4: `precomposedStringWithCanonicalMapping` on a million syllable string costs about 10 ms on this Mac, against 0.6 us for the head. Only the head the parse can read is normalized.
- Why the head is enough. The parse reads at most two scalars per block, an initial plus a pending araea being the only two scalar block, and it stops one block past the composition. A canonical decomposition of a precomposed syllable is at most three scalars, and every other scalar the parse accepts, a compatibility jamo or U+119E or U+11A2 or a lead jamo, has no canonical decomposition at all. Three scalars per block plus three therefore cover every block the parse can reach, and a cut past them can only shorten what the parse was going to stop at anyway.
- A pending araea is untouched by the normalization. U+1100 U+119E and U+1100 U+11A2 stay as they are, because Hangul canonical composition only joins a lead with a medial in U+1161 through U+1175.
- Non-vacuous: putting the scalar comparison back in a scratch copy fails exactly the new test.

- A candidate of a million scalars is also pinned by a test, accepted and rejected.
- No test kills the reverted parse, and none can: filtering and stopping return the same blocks, the difference is only cost. The table is the evidence.

## Minor 5, the Expansion initializer

- The explicit `public init(replacement:replacedLength:)` is removed. The matcher is the only thing that builds an `Expansion`, and the synthesized memberwise initializer is internal, which is all the matcher needs.
- `ShortcutMatcher.Entry.init` stays public. `LexiconPredictionProvider` calls it.
- The tests read the fields back through one local helper instead of constructing an expectation, which is what they can do under a plain `import HangulEngine`.
- The emitted module interface, release build with library evolution, now carries `Expansion` with its two public fields and no initializer. `matcher_replaced_word`, `matcher_nfc` and the other matcher mutants still die, so the field comparison did not weaken the suite.

- K5 keeps its `canExtend(to:)` passthrough and its `CompletionFilter` untouched. The engine now answers a decomposed candidate the way it answers the precomposed one, so the filter needs no normalization of its own, and the K5 measurement that the completer returns NFC still stands.
