## Known issues

Stated as behavior the owner and QA will see, not as branch or merge state. Each has a status-doc citation.

- Tapping inside a word that is still composing does not move the insertion point. The word commits and the next text lands at its end, not at the tap. `docs/status/I1.md`, third verdict, minor 1.
- The iPhone portrait keyboard frame is 17 points taller after returning from the background or switching keyboards, than on a fresh launch of the keyboard. This is system chrome the extension does not control, not a layout bug in the keyboard's own view. `docs/status/I1.md`, M3 section, "The portrait frame after a background return, measured."
- A chat-style field whose return handler clears the field on send no longer splits the syllable typed while the handler runs, measured whole in 6 of 6 rows at handler delays of 0.25, 0.5, and 0.8 seconds, where the previous build split 4 of 6. Two cases remain. A return handler that clears the field and returns true still splits the syllable in flight, since its clear cannot be told from a host clear. A key that lands in the few milliseconds between the handler's clear and the system's answer to the return, 0 to 5 milliseconds in the measurements with one 14 millisecond outlier, still splits there, so at 150 milliseconds per key a small share of sends hits it. `docs/status/K2.md` finding 9 and its Host limitations rows.
- After a host resign or a focus move, for example tapping into another field and back, a word still composing is no longer lost. It commits where the host left it, and the caret lands wherever the host's own refocus tap put it, not at a fixed position. `docs/status/K2.md` finding 6 measures this across `UITextField`, `UITextView`, and `UISearchBar`.
- In Safari on the iOS 27.0 simulator, where the system counts a hardware keyboard as connected, hiding the keyboard with its dismiss key leaves it hidden, and tapping the text field does not bring it back. From then on no app shows the keyboard until the simulator's minimization setting is reset. In UIKit text fields a tap after the dismiss key brings the keyboard back. A probe keyboard that only makes Apple's standard dismiss call behaves the same way in Safari. Behavior on a device is open until Q2. `docs/status/I1.md`, finding R07, `docs/status/K1.md`, "Dismiss key on the iOS 27.0 simulator," and `docs/status/Q1.md`, R07.

## Every 1.1 what's new claim, re-checked

The live 1.1 what's new text already says "Sound feedback, vibration feedback, and auto-suggestions can be turned off," so a 2.0 release note cannot claim any of those three toggles as new. Every line originally drafted for this release note is re-checked below against that text and against each status doc's own comparison to `archive/v1`, not against memory.

| Line | Verdict | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Key size can be set to small, medium, or large | Confirmed new | `docs/status/K1.md` records v1.1's key height as fixed at 56.0 points with no size setting. `docs/status/K8.md` adds the small/medium/large setting |
| The cycle-lock hold duration can be set from 0.5 to 2 seconds | Confirmed new | `docs/status/K3.md` greps every Swift file on `archive/v1` for `Timer`, `Date(`, `CACurrentMediaTime`, `DispatchTime`, `ContinuousClock`, `timeInterval` and `Clock` and finds none in the punctuation or consonant cycling path. 2.0 adds the cycle-lock window and its Settings stepper |
| The keyboard no longer requests Full Access | Confirmed new | `docs/status/P1.md` states v1.1 shipped `RequestsOpenAccess` true, flipped to `false` here per `PLAN.md`'s fixed points |
| Sound feedback can be turned on or off | Confirmed not new, and no longer accurate | The live 1.1 what's new already states this. `PLAN.md`'s Decisions table also removes the in-app sound toggle for 2.0 (`playInputClick()`, no in-app sound toggle), so 2.0 cannot claim this even as an unchanged feature |
| Haptic feedback can be turned on or off | Confirmed not new | The live 1.1 what's new already states this. `docs/status/H1.md`'s `isHapticFeedbackEnabled` key keeps the same toggle, and no status doc records a change to it |
| Word completions and phrase predictions can be turned on or off | Confirmed not new | The live 1.1 what's new already states auto-suggestions can be turned off. `docs/status/H1.md`'s `isPredictionEnabled` key keeps the same toggle, and no status doc records a change to it |
| A Text Replacement shortcut saved in Settings now shows in the first prediction slot | Not determinable from the listing | The live listing does not mention shortcuts or a prediction bar in either direction. No status doc compares this behavior to `archive/v1`. The feature exists in 2.0 (`docs/status/K4.md`, `docs/status/K6.md`) but this draft cannot say whether it is new |

Left out of the release note for the reasons in the table.

- The three toggle claims, confirmed not new by the live listing itself.
- The shortcut-in-slot-one claim, not determinable either way.
- Any claim that haptics or sound "feel" or "sound" better, or any specific claim about completion accuracy, since no status doc confirms either works on a physical device.
